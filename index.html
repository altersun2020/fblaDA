Formal vs informal

Two ways Americans give time. Formal volunteering = time given through an organization (school, food bank, faith group). Informal helping = exchanging favors with neighbors (house sitting, watching kids, lending tools). 2017–2023 CEV rates; grey dashed line is the older BLS formal-volunteering series (2003–2015), not comparable after the 2017 question redesign. Sources: BLS, AmeriCorps/Census CEV.
