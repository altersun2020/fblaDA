What Americans do for their communities

Every two years the Census Bureau asks 47,000 people what they did in the past year. Each card: the share who said yes in 2023, and how it moved since 2017.

Words to know. Behaviors are tagged formal (through an organization: volunteering, membership, giving), informal (neighbors: helping, talking, discussing, taking action), or other civic. "Points" = the plain difference between two percentages (23% → 28% is +5 points).
